My Editing Process

5 steps from raw footage to final delivery.

Every project follows the same structured pipeline — no guesswork, no surprises, no missed deadlines.

01

Discovery & Brief

I review your footage, script, references, target platform, and deadline. We align on tone, pacing, and deliverable formats before a single cut is made.

Creative BriefReference ReviewFormat Specs
02

Organisation & Proxy Setup

All footage is ingested, labelled, transcoded to proxy resolution, and synced. Multi-cam angles are matched and audio tracks are cleaned before assembly begins.

Proxy WorkflowMulti-cam SyncAudio Cleanup
03

Rough Cut & First Draft

I assemble the narrative structure, pacing, and hook. You receive a shareable review link within 48 hours — with frame-accurate commenting so feedback is precise.

Narrative Assembly48h TurnaroundReview Link
04

Polish: Colour, Motion & Sound

After approval I layer in the final grade, motion graphics, animated captions, custom SFX, music scoring, and voiceover equalization. This is where the magic happens.

Colour GradeMotion GraphicsSound Design
05

Export & Delivery

Final files are exported in all required formats — 4K master, platform-optimised versions, SRT subtitles, and a project archive. Delivered via cloud link within 24 hours of final sign-off.
