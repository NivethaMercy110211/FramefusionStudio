My Story

I didn't start with a studio.
I started with curiosity.

My journey into video editing began during university, where I started cutting short films and music videos purely for the love of storytelling. What began as a hobby quickly turned into a craft — I stayed up late learning keyboard shortcuts, studying color theory, and breaking down every frame of films I admired.

After freelancing on the side for two years, I made the leap to full-time post-production work. I've since delivered over 120 projects — from 10-second social clips to 30-minute documentary-style brand films — for content creators, marketing agencies, startups, and established brands across multiple industries.

Today, FrameFusion Studio is my creative base. Every project I take on reflects the same values I started with: honesty, craft, and obsession with the details most editors skip.

What makes my work different

  • Hook-first thinking

    I always start by asking: why should someone keep watching? Every edit leads with the strongest possible hook.

  • Music-driven pacing

    I edit to the beat — not just to match clips. The emotional arc of the music shapes every cut I make.

  • Design-aware editing

    With a background in Photoshop and Illustrator, I bring visual design thinking to every title card, lower third, and graphic element.

  • Clean, organized delivery

    You get labeled files, SRT subtitles, multi-format exports, and revision notes — every time, without being asked.
